Treadmill Running
Cardio
30
Start
at a low speed (6 km/h) to warm up, then gradually increase the speed based on your ability. Keep your back straight and look forward while running.
Chest Press
Strength
35
Sit
on the machine and hold the handles at chest level. Push forward until your arms are fully extended, then slowly return to the starting position.
Push-Ups
Strength
20
Place
your hands under your shoulders, keep your body straight. Lower your chest to the ground, then push up until arms are fully extended.
