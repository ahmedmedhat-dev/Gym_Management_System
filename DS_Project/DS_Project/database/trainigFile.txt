**Beginner Plan**

Duration: 4 weeks, 3 days a week

Goal: Get the body accustomed to exercise and improve general fitness

**Weekly Schedule:**

- **Day 1:**
    - Cardio exercises (15 minutes)
    - Push-ups – 3×10
    - Crunches – 3×15
    - Plank – 3×30 seconds
- **Day 2:**
    - Rest or light walking for 30 minutes
- **Day 3:**
    - Leg exercises (squats, lunges) – 3×12
    - Front pull – 3×10
    - Leg raises – 3×10
- **Day 4:**
    - Rest
- **Day 5:**
    - Zumba or spinning for 30 minutes
    - Dumbbell shoulder exercises – 3×10
    - Side plank – 3×30 seconds
- **Day 6 & 7:**
    - Rest

**Fat Loss Plan**

Duration: 4 weeks, 5 days a week

Goal: Boost metabolism, burn calories, and reduce fat

**Weekly Schedule:**

- **Day 1:**
    - HIIT for 25 minutes
    - Ab exercises (crunches, plank)
- **Day 2:**
    - Upper body resistance exercises – 3×12
    - Push-ups – 3×15
- **Day 3:**
    - Cardio (running or spinning for 40 minutes)
- **Day 4:**
    - Lower body resistance exercises (squats, lunges, jumps)
- **Day 5:**
    - Yoga or active recovery session
- **Day 6:**
    - Comprehensive exercises with light weights
- **Day 7:**
    - Rest

**Muscle Gain Plan**

Duration: 4 weeks, 6 days a week

Goal: Increase strength and muscle mass

**Weekly Schedule:**

- **Day 1:**
    - Chest and triceps (Bench Press, Chest Press, Triceps Pushdown)
- **Day 2:**
    - Back and biceps (Lat Pulldown, Chin-Ups, Rows)
- **Day 3:**
    - Legs (squats, lunges, Leg Press)
- **Day 4:**
    - Shoulders and core (Shoulder Press, Plank, Crunches)
- **Day 5:**
    - Comprehensive upper body and abs
- **Day 6:**
    - Legs and light cardio
- **Day 7:**
    - Rest
